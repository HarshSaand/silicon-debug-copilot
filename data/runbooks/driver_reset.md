# Driver reset triage

Runbook ID: `RB-RESET`

Use when a driver/device reset, recovery attempt, or reset loop is explicit.

1. Preserve the first failure before reset.
2. Count resets in the incident window.
3. Check whether initialization and health checks complete after recovery.
4. Correlate resets with timeout, link, firmware, and thermal evidence.
5. Escalate repeated resets or incomplete recovery.
